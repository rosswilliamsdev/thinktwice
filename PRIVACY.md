# ThinkTwice Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-28_

ThinkTwice is built to respect your privacy completely.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** ThinkTwice does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal
information, browsing history, or purchase data.

## Data handling

- ThinkTwice has **no backend server** and makes **no network requests**.
- The only data the extension stores is a single on/off setting, kept locally in
  your browser via `chrome.storage.sync`. This setting never leaves your Google
  account's own sync storage and is never sent to us or any third party.
- ThinkTwice does **not** track what you view, what you add to your cart, what
  you buy, or how much you spend.

## Permissions

- **`storage`** — used only to remember whether you've toggled the extension on
  or off.
- **Host access to `amazon.com`** — used only to remove fast-purchase buttons and
  show the cooling-off pause. The extension runs entirely in your browser on
  Amazon pages; it reads no data from them beyond what's needed to find those
  buttons.
